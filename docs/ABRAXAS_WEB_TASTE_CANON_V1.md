# ABRAXAS WEB TASTE CANON V1
*The Universal Aesthetic, Architectural & Interaction Canon for Digital Experiences*

---

## 1. Core Statement
> **ABRAXAS WEB EXPERIENCE = HIGH-END EDITORIAL PRODUCT FILM RENDERED AS AN INTERACTIVE SYSTEM.**

An ABRAXAS digital surface is never a generic SaaS dashboard, a neon cyber-cockpit, or a template card gallery. It is a cinematic, architecturally structured experience where spatial objects, typography, materials, lighting, and transitions work in concert to explain deep systems truthfully.

---

## 2. Universal Principles (Across Any Brand or Surface)

1. **Singular Monumental Concept**: Every digital surface centers on a clear conceptual protagonist rather than fragmented widgets.
2. **Editorial Hierarchy & Negative Space**: Typography sits directly in the composition in clean negative space. Never place text in dark backdrop boxes when composition and lighting can protect readability.
3. **Disciplined Color Strategy**: Every project establishes a disciplined dominant palette and restrained semantic competition appropriate to its identity.
4. **Physically Based Material Coherence & Color Management**: Explicitly manage color spaces (e.g. sRGB) and tone mapping when a physically based renderer is employed.
5. **Choreographed Progressive Disclosure**: Camera and viewport movements track narrative understanding. Spatial depth reveals structural layers only as the user explores.
6. **Forensic Provenance & Evidence Honesty**: Every product claim must be backed by verifiable engineering truth (commit SHAs, deterministic test assertions, reproducible health checks). Never use decorative fake data or randomized counters.
7. **Renderer Routing by Purpose**: Route to 3D/WebGL when spatial understanding is essential; fall back cleanly to accessible 2D vector schematics with fail-closed predictability.
8. **Anti-AI-Slop Restraint**: Avoid gratuitous particles, excessive neon glows, generic floating glass cards, and meaningless ambient animations.
9. **Accessibility & Reduced Motion**: Full support for keyboard navigation, screen reader landmarks, and instantaneous static snapping (`duration = 0`) under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
10. **Strict Performance Budgets**: Adaptive device pixel ratios (DPR), recursive resource disposal, and automatic loop pausing on background tabs.
