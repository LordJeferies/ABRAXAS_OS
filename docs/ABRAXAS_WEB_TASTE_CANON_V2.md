## 3. Universal Principles (Across Any Brand or Experience)

1. **One Scene = One Dominant Idea**: Every digital surface centers on a clear conceptual protagonist rather than fragmented widgets.
2. **Reduction is Luxury**: Omission creates prestige and focus. White/black negative space is active staging, not empty canvas. Never place text in dark backdrop boxes when composition and lighting can protect readability.
3. **Disciplined Dominant Palette**: Every experience establishes a strict dominant palette (80–90% primary base, 10–15% structural contrast, 2–5% semantic accents). Color is informational, never decorative.
4. **Physically Based Material Coherence**: Transparent surfaces must use true physical shading (roughness, metalness, view-dependent glancing Fresnel, and transmission) with explicit color management (ACES Filmic tone mapping, SRGB color space).
5. **Choreographed Progressive Disclosure**: Camera and viewport movements track narrative understanding. Spatial depth reveals structural layers only as the user explores.
6. **Forensic Provenance & Evidence Honesty**: Every product claim must be backed by verifiable engineering truth (commit SHAs, deterministic test assertions, reproducible health checks). Never use decorative fake data or randomized counters.
7. **Renderer Routing by Purpose**: Route to 3D/WebGL when spatial understanding is essential; fall back cleanly to accessible 2D vector schematics with fail-closed predictability.
8. **Anti-AI-Slop Restraint**: Avoid gratuitous particles, excessive neon glows, generic floating glass cards, and meaningless ambient animations.
9. **Accessibility & Reduced Motion**: Full support for keyboard navigation, screen reader landmarks, and instantaneous static snapping (`duration = 0`) under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
10. **Strict Performance Budgets**: Adaptive device pixel ratios (DPR $\le 2.0$), recursive Three.js resource disposal, and automatic animation loop pausing on background tabs.

## 4. Master Visual Symbols

Digital experiences authored by or for ABRAXAS subordinate their visual language around six primary symbols:

1. **THE PYRAMID**: The semantic spatial architecture of the system.
2. **THE LIENZO SPINE**: The persistent crystalline axis representing one content piece across all transformations.
3. **THE EYE OF ARQUITECTO**: Contextual observation, guidance, and architectural oversight (never horror-occult cliché).
4. **THE EXTERNAL WORLD**: The origin and destination of all client intent, real source ingress, platform distribution, and performance reality.
5. **THE HORIZON / THRESHOLD**: The physical boundary dividing abstract intent from manifested audiovisual reality.
6. **THE CONTENT PULSE / ARTIFACT**: The discrete content identity flowing through deterministic execution stages.
