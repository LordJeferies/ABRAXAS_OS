VAV

RELEASED (RC1)
Domain: Synthesis // Formation Cathedral // ⚝ Yetzirah / Subterranean Formation Cathedral (Tiferet synthesis, Netzach motion, Hod typography, Yesod integration)

Deterministic media synthesis forge: non-destructive video cuts, kinetic captions, and spring physics motions.

1. What It Is & Purpose

VAV is the audiovisual synthesis core of ABRAXAS OS. It transforms verified content and alignment data into broadcast-grade video masters across three synchronized tracks.

2. Spatial & Symbolic Association

Yetzirah / Subterranean Formation Cathedral (Tiferet synthesis, Netzach motion, Hod typography, Yesod integration)

This is an ABRAXAS visual and operational spatial association, not module identity. Tree states describe transformations of Contenido; modules act upon them.

3. Problem Solved

Eliminates destructive video re-encoding bottlenecks and manual subtitle styling errors via frame-accurate EDL compilation and programmatic layout engines.

4. Operational Example Flow

SHIM alignment received -> VAV Cuts trims silences via FFmpeg stream-copy -> VAV Captions formats kinetic hierarchy -> VAV Motions renders final master.

5. Strict Ownership Boundaries

✓ What VAV OWNS

  • Non-destructive video trimming
  • Word-level kinetic captions
  • Spring-physics motion layouts
  • Multi-segment EDL rendering

✗ What VAV DOES NOT OWN

  • Content strategy decisions (owned by YOD)
  • Social account credentials (owned by Publishing)
  • Task scheduling (owned by HE)

6. What It Is vs What It Is Not

What VAV IS What VAV IS NOT
A deterministic, local-first audiovisual production forge. Not a cloud transcoding SaaS wrapper, not an AI video hallucinator, and not an unconstrained video player.
