1. What It Is & Purpose

VAV converts resolved intent into audiovisual artifacts. Current verified areas must be represented truthfully; future production areas remain clearly classified as partial or planned.

3. Problem Solved

Replaces manual timeline editing in bloated video editors with programmatic, deterministic, frame-accurate audiovisual synthesis pipelines.

4. Operational Example Flow

Verified timecodes received -> VAV Cuts trims raw video via FFmpeg stream-copy -> VAV Captions formats kinetic words -> VAV Motions applies B-roll zooms -> Final video compiled.

5. Strict Ownership Boundaries

✓ What VAV OWNS

  • Non-destructive multi-segment video trimming (VAV/Cuts)
  • Word-level kinetic caption typography hierarchies (VAV/Captions)
  • Spring-physics motion layouts & B-roll transitions (VAV/Motions)
  • Remotion programmatic composition compilation
  • Stream-copy & master export pipeline execution

✗ What VAV DOES NOT OWN

  • Does NOT own script writing or hook criteria (YOD owns this)
  • Does NOT own reality observation or coverage scoring (SHIM owns this)
  • Does NOT own approval statuses (HE owns this)
  • Does NOT own cloud social account publishing (Publishing owns this)
