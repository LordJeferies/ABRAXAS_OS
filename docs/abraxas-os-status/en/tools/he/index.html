1. What It Is & Purpose

HE translates content architectures into operable human workflows: managing recording sessions, talent deadlines, role boundaries, and QA approvals.

2. Spatial & Symbolic Association

3. Problem Solved

Eliminates chaotic task fragmentation across disparate apps by uniting content state directly with deadline calendars and approval checklists.

4. Operational Example Flow

Recording scheduled -> Talent prompt pack compiled in HE I -> Recording session executed -> Master approved in HE II.

5. Strict Ownership Boundaries

✓ What HE OWNS

  • Task and deadline lifecycles
  • Solo & Team operational modes
  • Recording session scheduling
  • QA review and approval gates

✗ What HE DOES NOT OWN

  • Phonetic discrepancy analysis (owned by SHIM)
  • Timeline rendering and frame composition (owned by VAV)
  • Content DAG storage (owned by Contenido)

6. What It Is vs What It Is Not

What HE IS What HE IS NOT
A deterministic operational governance desk for human and team workflows. Not a video editor, not a generative AI model, and not an unconstrained social network.
