Shim

Real-Source Observation & Candidate Resolution

Observes real media sources, performs checksum verification, resolves candidate assets, and ensures evidence integrity.

01. What & Why

Definition: The reality threshold separating planned intent from observed and resolved source footage.

Why It Exists: Ensures that the production pipeline never fabricates missing footage—detecting gaps truthfully and routing them to pickup sessions.

02. Ownership Boundaries

✓ Owns

  • Raw media ingest & SHA-256 verification
  • Speech-to-text transcript timestamp alignment
  • Coverage matching against planned beats
  • Truthful gap detection (FOUND vs MISSING)
  • Explicit editor beat resolution locking

✗ Does Not Own

  • Does NOT invent missing content or hallucinate takes
  • Does NOT own strategic hook planning (YOD owns this)
  • Does NOT own master video cutting (VAV owns this)

03. Contracts & Data Flow

Inputs:
  • Raw audio/video takes
  • Transcript segments
  • Planned beats from Lienzo
Outputs:
  • ObservationReport
  • GapEvent
  • ResolvedLayer
Example Lifecycle Flow:

Raw take ingested -> Transcript aligned -> Beat 1 & 2 FOUND, Beat 3 MISSING -> Gap event emitted -> Pickup recorded -> Beat 3 resolved.

04. Events & Produced Lineage

Emitted Events:
  • SHIM_SOURCE_INGESTED
  • SHIM_GAP_DETECTED
  • SHIM_BEATS_RESOLVED
Produced Artifacts:
  • ObservationManifest
  • ResolvedBeatMap

05. Capability Truth & Roadmap

Current Capabilities

  • Checksum Contracts
