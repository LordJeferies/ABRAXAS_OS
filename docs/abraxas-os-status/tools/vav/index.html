VAV

Audiovisual Synthesis, Cuts & Motions Engine

Executes non-destructive multi-segment cutting via local FFmpeg, frame-accurate Remotion motion compositions, caption compilation, and platform safe-zone validations.

01. What & Why

Definition: The deterministic audiovisual embodiment forge where structured intent becomes master video.

Why It Exists: Replaces slow manual timeline editing with programmatic, non-destructive, frame-accurate FFmpeg cuts and Remotion motion graphics.

02. Ownership Boundaries

✓ Owns

  • Non-destructive multi-segment video timeline assembly
  • Lossless master stream isolation & FFmpeg execution
  • Kinetic motion graphics & typography composition
  • Platform 9:16 safe-zone collision avoidance
  • Frame-accurate Remotion rendering

✗ Does Not Own

  • Does NOT own content strategy (YOD owns this)
  • Does NOT own content identity (Lienzo owns this)
  • Does NOT own editorial approval decisions (He owns this)

03. Contracts & Data Flow

Inputs:
  • Resolved beat timestamps from SHIM
  • Typography style rules
  • Motion preset definitions
Outputs:
  • CutArtifact
  • CaptionArtifact
  • MotionArtifact
  • MasterVideoMP4
Example Lifecycle Flow:

Resolved timestamps received -> FFmpeg cuts jump-points -> Safe-zone captions styled -> Remotion motion rendered -> Master video outputted.
