Lienzo

Persistent Content Identity & Authoring Canvas

Provides persistent, editable, versioned Source of Truth per content piece with full before/after revision history.

01. What & Why

Definition: The persistent, editable, versioned Source of Truth for one content piece across its entire lifecycle.

Why It Exists: Guarantees that a piece of content preserves its unique identity across plans, raw takes, edits, renders, and publications without state drift.

02. Ownership Boundaries

✓ Owns

  • Unique content identity (contentId)
  • Component DAG & Layer separation (STRATEGY, PLANNED, OBSERVED, RESOLVED, PRODUCTION)
  • Revision CAS & exclusive locking
  • Component version increments (v+1) on update
  • Impact Engine & OUT_OF_SYNC dependency invalidation
  • Traceable before/after revision history

✗ Does Not Own

  • Does NOT own global audience intelligence (Yod owns this)
  • Does NOT own human team assignments or blocker status (He owns this)
  • Does NOT own video rendering execution (VAV owns this)

03. Contracts & Data Flow

Inputs:
  • Strategic beats from YOD
  • Resolved segments from SHIM
  • Production artifacts from VAV
Outputs:
  • LienzoRevision
  • ImpactReport
  • Synchronized Content State
Example Lifecycle Flow:

YOD plan initializes Lienzo rev 1 -> SHIM resolves takes to rev 2 -> VAV cut attached to rev 3 -> Upstream edit triggers OUT_OF_SYNC impact on rev 4.
