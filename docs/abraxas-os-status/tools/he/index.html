He

Operations Core, Tasks, Teams & Workflows

Manages tasks, assignments, dependencies, operational blockers, Kanban projections, production calendars, recording sessions, approvals, time tracking, and notification center.

01. What & Why

Definition: The operational window and governance core orchestrating human teams and production workflows.

Why It Exists: Provides strict RBAC, deadline enforcement, blocker resolution trees, and multi-role review governance for media execution.

02. Ownership Boundaries

✓ Owns

  • Tasks, assignments & Kanban state projections
  • Typed blocker dependencies & resolution trees
  • Recording sessions & talent booking calendar
  • Multi-role approval gates & edit locks
  • Time tracking & stopwatch telemetry
  • Notification center & activity ledgers

✗ Does Not Own

  • Does NOT own YOD criteria or strategic intelligence
  • Does NOT own Lienzo content state (He projects and operates it)
  • Does NOT own Publishing distribution targets or Metrics evidence

03. Contracts & Data Flow

Inputs:
  • Lienzo state projections
  • Team availability
  • Talent booking requirements
Outputs:
  • TaskState
  • ApprovalDecision
  • RecordingSession
  • NotificationEvent
Example Lifecycle Flow:

Lienzo recording requirement creates Task -> Talent assigned -> Session scheduled on Calendar -> Recording complete -> Approval gate triggered.
