## 1. Executive Summary & Art Direction Philosophy

The **ABRAXAS Spatial Pyramid** is a persistent, interactive, 3D semantic spatial architecture engineered specifically for **Public Status V3**. It serves as the primary visual protagonist for progressive technical storytelling, explaining:
- Systemic module ownership and non-negotiable boundaries.
- The complete lifecycle of a single content piece.
- Current verified capabilities vs planned target features.
- Dynamic feedback loops and forensic activity provenance.

The Spatial Pyramid is **not decorative filler** and **not a universal requirement for future client sites**—it is a specialized, high-craft architectural visualization designed exclusively for the ABRAXAS OS Public Status experience.

## 2. Symbolic Origin Context & Conceptual Transformation Grammar

### A. Symbolic Origin Context (Art Direction / Conceptual Foundation)
The conceptual transformation sequence of the Spatial Pyramid is intentionally derived by the system author from an esoteric Christian-Kabbalistic reading of the transformational sequence:
$$\text{YOD} \rightarrow \text{HE I} \rightarrow \text{SHIN-position / SHIM} \rightarrow \text{VAV} \rightarrow \text{HE II}$$
corresponding to the Pentagrammaton ($Y-H-Sh-W-H$) and symbolic Christification of primordial intent into concrete worldly manifestation.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Separation of Art Direction from Technical Architecture**:
> 1. This symbolic narrative is **conceptual art-direction context** that explains *why* the spatial geometry and transformation chambers exist in this specific arrangement.
> 2. It is **not** theological dogma and **not** normative Judaism.
> 3. Canonical runtime module names remain unchanged: the reality-observation module is canonically **`SHIM`**.
> 4. Symbolic concepts **never** override strict software engineering boundaries, role-based access control, or domain ownership invariants.

### B. Core Interpretation Matrix
- **`YOD` (Originating Seed / Intelligence)**:
  The transcendent apex of pure intent, strategic criteria, audience modeling, hook patterns, and opportunity generation before physical execution begins.
- **`HE I` (The First Window / Window of Intent)**:
  The upper operational portal through which abstract intent is first structured, scheduled, and made visible to human teams (tasks, deadlines, recording sessions, assignments, pre-flight approvals).
- **`SHIM` (The Transformative Threshold / Encounter with Reality)**:
  The central transverse membrane where planned conceptual beats encounter real-world physical footage and audio takes. Shim truthfully observes, matches, and detects gaps without fabricating missing reality.
- **`VAV` (The Connecting Bridge / Embodiment Forge)**:
  The materializing bridge where structured, oniric, and semantic intent is forged into concrete audiovisual substance (precision jump-cuts, kinetic motion typography, Remotion compositions, and pixel rendering).
- **`HE II` (The Second Window / Window of Manifestation)**:
  The lower operational observatory through which completed work is verified (QA), released, distributed across multi-platform publishing targets, and monitored via live performance metrics.
- **`ARQUITECTO EYE` (Transcendent Awareness & Guidance)**:
  The procedural optical eye hovering above the pyramid, observing all chambers and casting guidance rays without claiming ownership of underlying domain truth.

## 3. Chamber Specifications & Structural Roles

### A. YOD — The Intelligence Apex
- **Spatial Position**: The luminous apex of the pyramid.
- **Role**: Source of forensic criteria, hook intelligence, structural patterns, client core parameters, and opportunity generation.
- **Inputs**: Client Core & Brand Intelligence entering from exterior portals.
- **Visual Representation**: Luminous crystalline apex casting downward illumination into the structure.

### B. HE I — The Window of Intent (Pre-Production)
- **Spatial Position**: Upper front chamber beneath YOD.
- **Role**: Operational visibility and governance of pre-production intent.
- **Projected Entities**: Client, Opportunity, Plan, Lienzo initialization, Recording Packs, Recording Sessions, Pre-production Tasks, Assignments, Dependencies, Deadlines, Calendar, Pre-flight Approvals.
- **Invariant**: He governs and schedules; He does not own YOD intelligence or Lienzo content truth.
- **Visual Representation**: Precision mechanical titanium ring and holographic task lattices framing the upper Lienzo spine.

### C. SHIM — The Reality Chamber (The Threshold)
- **Spatial Position**: Transverse optical membrane slicing across the middle interior of the pyramid.
- **Role**: Observation and resolution against real-world source footage.
- **Process Sequence**: $\text{PLANNED} \rightarrow \text{OBSERVED} \rightarrow \text{RESOLVED}$.
- **Projected Entities**: Raw source takes, transcript segments, candidate matches, truthful gap markers ($P1\text{ FOUND}, P2\text{ FOUND}, P3\text{ MISSING}$), evidence references, explicit editor resolution.
- **Invariant**: Shim **never invents missing source material**. Gaps are explicitly marked and routed to pickup tasks.
- **Visual Representation**: Translucent iridescent Fresnel membrane with split-view comparison planes and gap warning chevrons.

### D. VAV — The Embodiment Chamber (The Audiovisual Forge)
- **Spatial Position**: Lower interior production chamber beneath the Shim membrane.
- **Role**: Where structured semantic intent becomes concrete audiovisual media.
- **Projected Entities**: Non-destructive FFmpeg cut timelines, Remotion motion graphics layers, kinetic caption safe zones, typography presets, frame rendering engines, composition tiles.
- **Invariant**: VAV is the execution engine, not strategic truth.
- **Visual Representation**: High-energy horizontal laser timeline rails, animated waveform ribbons, and dynamic composition bounding boxes.

### E. HE II — The Window of Manifestation (Post-Production)
- **Spatial Position**: Lower front observatory overlooking outward distribution ports.
- **Role**: Operational visibility and governance of post-production execution, QA review, and multi-channel publication.
- **Projected Entities**: Master video outputs, QA approval gates, edit locks, release tasks, publication targets, distribution calendar, performance alerts.
- **Source of Truth Invariant**: He displays and operates Publishing and Metrics, but **does NOT become their Source of Truth**. Publishing owns distribution targets; Metrics owns performance evidence.
- **Visual Representation**: Wide panoramic observation deck with outward portal viewports and telemetry monitoring consoles.

## 8. Four Status Experience Modes

The Public Status V3 interface operates in four distinct user modes:

1. **STORY MODE**:
   - The guided cinematic narrative following the symbolic $\text{YOD} \rightarrow \text{HE I} \rightarrow \text{SHIM} \rightarrow \text{VAV} \rightarrow \text{HE II}$ journey with synchronized camera states (S0–S11).
2. **SYSTEM MODE**:
   - Free interactive 3D spatial exploration. Users can pan, orbit, and inspect individual chambers and module dossiers.
   - Includes a prominent **CURRENT vs TARGET** toggle switch:
     - `CURRENT`: Renders only verified local capabilities (`RELEASED_RC1` / `VERIFIED_LOCAL`).
     - `TARGET`: Renders full envisioned target architecture with clear ghosted blueprints for future modules.
3. **FLOW MODE (Pipeline Visualizer)**:
   - Interactive DAG visualizer allowing visitors to select a **Pipeline Blueprint** (e.g., `CORE_LOOP_FULL_V1`, `VAV_STANDARD_VIDEO_V1`) and trace the exact path of data execution across chambers.
   - Displayed visibly: `DESIGN BLUEPRINT — NOT RUNTIME EXECUTABLE`.
4. **PROOF MODE**:
   - Evidence-first verification view prioritizing concrete Remotion render stills, He browser runtime captures, and FFmpeg unit/integration test logs over 3D graphics.

## 10. Deep Module Dossier Specification

Selecting any module in System Mode opens a deep, structured dossier following the standard schema:
1. **WHAT**: Concise architectural definition.
2. **WHY**: Strategic purpose in the content lifecycle.
3. **OWNS**: Exclusive responsibilities and state boundaries.
4. **DOES_NOT_OWN**: Non-responsibilities and prohibited data duplications.
5. **INPUTS**: Valid upstream contracts and data requirements.
6. **OUTPUTS**: Produced artifacts and emitted events.
7. **CURRENT STATUS**: Verified local implementation status (`VERIFIED_LOCAL`, `PARTIAL_FOUNDATION`, `CONTRACT_ONLY`, `PLANNED`).
8. **TARGET ARCHITECTURE**: Envisioned post-RC1 evolutionary roadmap.
9. **SYSTEM CONNECTIONS**: Upstream and downstream module dependencies.
10. **EVENT FOOTPRINT**: Typed events emitted into the Backbone ledger.
11. **ARTIFACT FOOTPRINT**: Registered immutable artifacts and hashes.
12. **EXAMPLE FLOW**: Concrete step-by-step lifecycle walkthrough.
13. **CONCRETE EVIDENCE**: Associated test files, build verification, and render stills.
14. **BOUNDED DEBT**: Known limitations and planned refinements.
15. **ROADMAP GATES**: Associated milestones from `roadmap.json`.
