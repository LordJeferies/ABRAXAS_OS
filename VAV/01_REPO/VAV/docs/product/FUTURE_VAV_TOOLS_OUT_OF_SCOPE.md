# Future VAV Tools — explicitly OUT of Vav-captions

Do NOT add these to Vav-captions now:

- AI avatar
- image generator
- stock media
- filters
- color grading
- background removal
- video transitions
- beat editing
- B-roll generator
- music library
- general clip cutting
- noise removal suite
- recording studio
- livestream

Reason:
Vav-captions must remain a specialized caption/transcript/composition tool.

These ideas may become later VAV modules/tools.
Do not assign implementation ownership prematurely unless a future product
decision explicitly creates that module.

Explicit existing boundary:
general clip cutting belongs outside Vav-captions; Vav-cuts remains future scope.
