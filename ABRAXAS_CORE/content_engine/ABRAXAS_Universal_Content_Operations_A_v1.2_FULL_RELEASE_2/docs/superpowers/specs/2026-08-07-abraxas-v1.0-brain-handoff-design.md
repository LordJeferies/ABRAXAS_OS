# ABRAXAS A v1.0 · Brain Navigator 2.0 + Handoff Release

## Goal
Ship a stable v1.0 that preserves the v0.9.6.1 functional recovery, makes the procedural brain the unmistakable primary Home visual, introduces user-controlled brain zoom from 1× to 2×, improves tool hotspots with leader lines/icons/popovers, and packages enough context/prompts/roadmap for v1.1 to continue in another chat.

## Brain Navigator 2.0
- 1× exactly represents the v0.9.6.1 baseline size. User can zoom to 2×; no smaller than 1× and no larger than 2×.
- Zoom applies around the same brain center, updates particle targets and the hotspot overlay together, and persists in local UI state.
- Controls show −, current percentage, +, plus Reset. They are visible on Home Visual only.
- Primary form is the brain. Brain target receives denser particles and a subtle procedural silhouette/groove guide; other scroll targets remain routes/queue/calendar/network.
- Six tool hotspots remain: He, Shim, Production, Calendar, Assets, Brand Intelligence.
- Every hotspot shows a visible node, leader line, and tool symbol. Hover/focus opens a compact floating popover with name, purpose and action.

## Liquid Glass overlay rule
Home copy, brain status and zoom controls sit on translucent functional material; the brain must remain perceptible through the surfaces. No opaque card may cover the primary brain region. Content sections lower on the page remain stable dark surfaces.

## Continuity / handoff
The FULL RELEASE must include: source, standalone, tests, reports, reference assets, master project context, visual direction prompt, v1.1 continuation prompt, visual/motion JSON, roadmap, decision log, release notes and explicit next-version brief.
